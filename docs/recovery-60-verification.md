## Gaps closed by #75

1. **Admission record** (was: no coordinator generation). `board/admission.mts` keeps `<state>/admission/state.json`: claims with tokens and owner process identities (pid plus start time), per-project activity generations, and recovery attempt counters with pending charges, under one interprocess lock that is never taken over by age or a live pid alone; 0700/0600, fsync'd temp-file rename, corrupt or unknown versions fail closed. `claimRun` and `claimRecovery` admit through it; a recovery claim and its attempt charge commit in one replacement, and a crash before the charge reaches the recovery record is replayed once (`cli/test/recovery-claim.test.mts`, with a claimant process that exits between the writes). Normal admission advances the generation, which breaks a pending jam confirmation.
2. **Claims after the worker exits** (was: the run record's `hold`). The `hold` field is removed. A recovery claim lives in the admission record whatever its record's stage or process, keeps the ticket, a capacity slot and the whole project, and is released only by `settleRecovery` after the exact container is confirmed gone (`cli/test/recovery-run.test.mts`, `orchestrator/recovery-entrypoint.test.mts`). Board writes and exit handlers are token-fenced, so a stale heartbeat cannot overwrite a newer claim.
3. **Ownership receipts and remote-only branches.** `orchestrator/ownership.mts` writes `<state>/ownership/<projectId>/<iid>.json` before a branch or worktree is created (normal `main.mts` runs and the recovery launcher) and fills identities in as they complete; `resumeTarget` checks them and reconciles a crash between receipt and creation. Pre-upgrade stopped runs are accepted only on the exact recorded branch with the one canonical worktree and a matching common directory, then adopted into a receipt. A remote-only recorded branch is fetched exactly and created locally without force; several matching refs or a mismatch are refused. `factory retry` keeps receipts and refuses cards protected by active or unknown claims. Records are written atomically at 0600 with fsync.
4. **Prerequisite holds before delivery.** `prerequisiteHold` rechecks, before verification and again before delivery, the dependencies the dispatcher's analysis records; one in flight or in an open PR holds the attempt, and unknown (no or stale analysis, a failed lookup) blocks delivery.
